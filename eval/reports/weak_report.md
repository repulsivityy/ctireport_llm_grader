# Monthly Cyber Threat Update — August

Prepared by the security team.

## Overview

This month there was a lot of cyber activity affecting companies around the
world. Ransomware is still a big problem and many organisations got hit. There
were also some new vulnerabilities in popular software that attackers are
using. Phishing emails continue to be sent in large numbers and some employees
clicked on them.

We saw news about a few large breaches in the retail and healthcare sectors.
The attackers seem to be getting more sophisticated and are using AI now. Some
reports say that attacks have gone up compared to last year, maybe by quite a
lot. Nation state actors are also active and could be targeting critical
infrastructure, though it is hard to say for sure.

## Technical Details

Attackers used various TTPs including phishing, exploitation of public facing
applications, PowerShell, credential dumping with mimikatz, lateral movement
over SMB, and data exfiltration to cloud storage. Some malware families
observed include common loaders and info-stealers. C2 was over HTTPS. There
were also brute force attempts against RDP and VPN portals on the perimeter.

Indicators change frequently so they are not listed here.

## What This Means

Cyber threats are a serious risk to every organisation and the situation may
get worse over time. Our environment could be affected if we are not careful.
It is possible that we could be targeted given our industry.

## Recommendations

- Stay vigilant and keep monitoring for threats.
- Make sure antivirus and EDR are up to date.
- Patch systems regularly.
- Train staff on phishing awareness.
- Review firewall rules.
- Keep backups.
